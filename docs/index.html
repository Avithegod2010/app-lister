Features

Small app. Serious attention to detail.

Everything you need to browse your apps — and not a single thing more.

📋

Complete list

Every installed package with its real icon, name, package id and version — user and system apps alike.

🔍

Instant search

Filter by app name or package name as you type. Results update on every keystroke.

↕️

Six sort orders

A–Z, Z–A, newest and oldest installs, recently updated, or largest APK — with sticky letter headers.

▦

List & grid

A dense list for details, a 4-column grid for browsing. One tap to switch, your choice remembered.

ℹ️

Deep details

Version, version code, min & target Android, install and update dates, APK size and path.

🚀

Quick actions

Launch any app, jump to its Android App Info page, or copy its package name — right from the details.

📤

Export anywhere

Share the whole list as text, CSV or JSON via the share sheet, or copy it straight to the clipboard.

🎨

Material You

Dynamic color generated from your wallpaper, full light & dark themes, springs, shimmer and haptics.

⚡

Fast by design

Icons cached to disk, scans streamed with live progress, 60fps rendering with FlashList.

Privacy

It's nobody's business what apps you have.

App Lister contains no ads, no trackers, no analytics and no network access of any kind. Your app list is read once, shown beautifully, and never leaves the device — exports go only where you share them.

🔌 No internet permission used 🕵️ Zero trackers 🔐 One permission: app list only 📄 Open source, MIT
